A welcome from our Head

A small school with high expectations.

Kilele means "summit" in Kiswahili. We chose the name because we believe every child has a peak of their own to reach, and that our job is to walk beside them on the way up.

Our teachers know each pupil by name, by strength and by struggle. We keep classes small so that reading gets heard, questions get answered and quiet children get noticed. Alongside strong results in English, Kiswahili and Mathematics, we care just as much about honesty, kindness and courage.

The best way to understand Kilele is to visit on an ordinary school morning. I would be delighted to show you around.

Grace LyimoHead Teacher, B.Ed (UDSM), M.Ed (Leadership)
Curriculum

Learning that builds, year on year.

We teach the Tanzanian national curriculum in English, with Kiswahili taught to a high standard and French from Standard III.

Confident first steps

Two years of play-based learning in bright, safe classrooms. Children learn letters and sounds, early number, and how to share, listen and take turns, so they arrive in Standard I ready and excited.

  • Phonics & early reading
  • Number & shape
  • Kiswahili songs & stories
  • Art & free play
  • Music & movement
  • Outdoor discovery
CLASS SIZEUp to 20 children

Fluent readers, careful thinkers

Pupils become fluent readers and writers in English and Kiswahili, master the four operations and begin hands-on science. Each child reads aloud to an adult every day.

  • English
  • Kiswahili
  • Mathematics
  • Science & Technology
  • French (from Std III)
  • Arts & Sports
